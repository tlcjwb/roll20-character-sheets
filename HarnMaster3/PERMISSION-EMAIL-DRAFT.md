**Subject:** Permission to include HârnMaster text in a free, non-commercial fan tool

Hello,

I'm a HârnMaster GM and a customer of Columbia Games — I own HârnMaster (4001), HârnMaster Magic
(4301) and its Fyvria expansion (4341), HârnMaster Religion (4401), HârnWorld Bestiary (4601),
Ivashu (4611), and Ostlers (4823), among others.

I'm building a **free, non-commercial fan tool** for running HârnMaster on Roll20: a character
sheet, a local character generator, and an importer that builds a character per the HârnMaster
rules. Today the tool ships **only functional game mechanics** (attribute scores, skill OMLs,
occupation/skill bundles, etc.) and deliberately keeps **all descriptive prose out of the public
distribution** — descriptions are loaded only from the owner's own copy at runtime, never bundled.

I'd like to ask permission to **include descriptive text** — skill descriptions, ritual and
invocation descriptions, and spell descriptions from 4001, 4301, and 4401 — so the tool can show
them alongside the mechanics as a convenience to players who own the books.

What prompts me to ask: Columbia already distributes a free HârnMaster character generator that
includes such descriptions verbatim, so I wanted to check whether fan tools may do likewise — and
whether there's an existing fan-content license that already covers this.

I'm glad to honor whatever terms you prefer: strictly **non-commercial and free**, full
**copyright/attribution notices**, scope limits, or any conditions you'd set. If there's a fan-use
license or policy I should follow, please point me to it.

Thank you for HârnMaster — it's a remarkable system, and I appreciate your long support of fan
projects.

Best regards,
